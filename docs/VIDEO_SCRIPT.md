# MarketPulse — 4-Minute Demo Video Script

**Total runtime:** 4:00 · **Narration:** ~500 words (≈130 wpm, calm presentation pace)
**Format:** screen recording + voiceover. One scene = one camera move.
**Golden rule:** let each scene settle 1–2 seconds before speaking. Silence reads as confidence.

---

## Scene 1 — The Hook (0:00 – 0:20)

**On screen:** Intro slide on black: *"What if your market app showed you its math?"* → cross-fade into the live Overview page.

**Narration (~40 words):**
> Every market app gives you a price. Almost none tell you *why* it matters — and the few that use AI hide it in a black box. MarketPulse is different: an AI decision-support dashboard for the Indian market, with zero black box.

**Camera move:** slow zoom-in on the NIFTY card as the live price ticks.

---

## Scene 2 — Live Market Board (0:20 – 0:45)

**On screen:** Overview page — scroll slowly across the grid, then point out the "● live prices · updated HH:MM:SS" chip.

**Narration (~55 words):**
> The whole board is live. NIFTY, SENSEX, global indices, commodities, currencies — forty-six instruments refreshing in real time, layered over official exchange data. And down here — that timestamp? It updates by itself, every minute. This isn't a screenshot; the market is breathing on the screen.

**Camera move:** two slow scrolls; hover a card so its tooltip shows.

---

## Scene 3 — Open a Stock (0:45 – 1:15)

**On screen:** Search overlay → type "tcs" → open the TCS page. Let the intraday chart and the big live price land first.

**Narration (~65 words):**
> Search any listed company — here's TCS. The big number is the *live* price, updating every minute, with the previous official close right underneath. The chart opens on the live five-minute view and keeps streaming while you watch — daily and ten-year views are one tab away. But the real product starts below the price.

**Camera move:** type visibly, pause, scroll to the verdict card.

---

## Scene 4 — The Math Engine (1:15 – 1:50)

**On screen:** The verdict + Evidence panel — show trend/momentum/volatility/volume rows, then the equation line.

**Narration (~75 words):**
> This verdict is not an opinion — it's an equation. A deterministic math engine scores Trend, Momentum, Volatility and Volume — twenty indicators in total — each with its rule, its weight, and its contribution visible right here. Add news sentiment, capped at ten percent, and you get a zero-to-one-hundred score. Anyone can audit it. That's our core promise: the math decides, and you can check every step.

**Camera move:** scroll down the evidence rows one by one; end on the equation.

---

## Scene 5 — Where the AI Comes In (1:50 – 2:25)

**On screen:** The AI thesis block — highlight the four sections, then select one sentence → Pulse Assistant popup explains it.

**Narration (~80 words):**
> So where's the AI? Not in guessing numbers — in *translating* them. Gemini receives our computed features and writes a structured thesis: structural read, cross-asset drivers, risk conditions, and what would change this read. And here's the crucial part — every number it writes is programmatically checked against the data payload. If the model ever invents a figure, the output is rejected and replaced. Advice and predictions are banned — and enforced in code, not just in the prompt. Select any line, and the Pulse Assistant re-explains it in simple words.

**Camera move:** highlight text with the cursor; the PA popup appears on click.

---

## Scene 6 — News & Sentiment (2:25 – 2:45)

**On screen:** News page — show publisher chips and a sentiment tag.

**Narration (~45 words):**
> News flows in live from Google News with publisher attribution, and each headline gets a sentiment score that gently nudges the verdict. The narrative gets a voice — but the math always stays in charge.

**Camera move:** scroll through 3–4 headlines slowly.

---

## Scene 7 — The PRISM Story (2:45 – 3:35) ⭐ the differentiator

**On screen:** Split or cut to the PRISM dashboard: the old 27/100 audit screenshot → then a fresh 90+ trace.

**Narration (~105 words):**
> Now, how do we *prove* the AI is behaving? Every AI call in MarketPulse is traced to PRISM — an independent AI-observability dashboard. It scores quality, response and compliance, and measures latency for each call. PRISM audited us and scored one output twenty-seven out of one hundred. We treated every deduction as a bug report: a fabricated number led to a grounding guard; format drift led to a format enforcer; truncated outputs led to automatic retries. The AI got measurably better — and the audit loop never stops, so quality can't silently regress. This is the same discipline a regulated, education-grade product needs.

**Camera move:** show the audit screenshot for a beat, then the improved score.

---

## Scene 8 — Close (3:35 – 4:00)

**On screen:** Overview page pulling back, or the About page's "What MarketPulse is NOT" card.

**Narration (~55 words):**
> MarketPulse: live Indian-market data from the exchange itself, explainable math you can audit, honest AI with guardrails on every path, and observability built in from day one. It will never tell you what to buy — it makes sure you understand *why* the market is what it is. MarketPulse. Educational information — not investment advice.

**Camera move:** fade to the logo / repo URL card. Hold 3 seconds of silence.

---

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Record at 1080p+, browser zoomed to ~110%, dark theme (matches branding)
- [ ] Close other tabs/notifications; use Win+G (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS
- [ ] Fresh reload before Scene 2 so the "updated HH:MM:SS" chip is visible
- [ ] Have the PRISM before/after screenshots ready as image slides for Scene 7
- [ ] Record voice separately or together; speak slower than feels natural — aim 130 wpm
- [ ] Total narration is ~500 words → leaves ~25s of breathing room across 4 minutes
